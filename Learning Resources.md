# Learning Resources

Every resource the program uses, mapped to the sprint that needs it. Nothing here is assigned as a watchlist. A resource is assigned against a specific task or it is not assigned.

Cost marked as **free**, **paid**, or **library** (worth borrowing rather than buying).

---

## 1. Assignment rule

A resource is read to solve the task in front of the designer. Sprint 1 does not read the design systems book. Sprint 4 does. Reading ahead is allowed and never required.

Three tiers:

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Core** | Assigned. Read it in the sprint listed. Defense questions assume it. |
| **Reference** | Open it when a task needs it. Not read cover to cover. |
| **Optional** | For whoever wants depth or a specialisation. Never graded. |

## 8. The fundamentals spine reading, all 24 weeks

Runs under every sprint. One why session per week, presented by a rotating designer.

| Weeks | Topic | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Gestalt, hierarchy, whitespace | Refactoring UI, IxDF Gestalt articles |
| 3 to 4 | Typography and type systems | Practical Typography, Thinking with Type |
| 5 to 6 | Cognition: memory, attention, load | 100 Things Every Designer Needs to Know About People, Weinschenk |
| 7 to 8 | Mental models, affordances, signifiers | The Design of Everyday Things |
| 9 to 10 | Behavioural economics in interfaces | behavioraleconomics.com free guide, Laws of UX |
| 11 to 12 | Ethics, dark patterns, regulation | deceptive.design, DPDP Act summary, EU DSA deceptive design provisions |
| 13 to 14 | Systems thinking and abstraction cost | Design Systems, Kholmatova |
| 15 to 16 | Web platform: semantics, CSS, performance | web.dev Learn, MDN, Core Web Vitals docs |
| 17 to 18 | Probabilistic systems, trust calibration, automation bias | PAIR Guidebook, HAX Toolkit |
| 19 to 20 | Accessibility depth: screen readers, cognitive access | Inclusive Components, A11y Project, Deque University |
| 21 to 22 | Measurement, significance, and its abuse | Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments, Lean Analytics |
| 23 to 24 | Communication and decision defense | Articulating Design Decisions, Discussing Design |

**Teardown subjects, one per week.** Assigned by admin, drawn from products with real constraints: a government service portal, a bank app, an insurance claim flow, an airline booking, a hospital appointment system, a food delivery checkout, a UPI payment confirmation, an enterprise admin console, an AI assistant surface, a subscription cancellation flow. Cancellation flows and consent surfaces are assigned deliberately because that is where dark patterns live.

## 10. Adjacent tools: when they matter and when they do not

Asked for directly, answered honestly. None of these are required to clear a product design interview loop. Each has a narrow case where it is the right tool.

| Tool | Cost | Genuinely needed when | Not needed for | Learn from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Adobe Photoshop** | Paid | Photo retouching for real marketing or ecommerce imagery, complex masking, print prep | Any UI work. Figma covers interface design entirely. | Adobe official tutorials, Phlearn free tutorials for retouching |
| **Adobe Illustrator** | Paid | Custom icon sets, logo work, complex vector illustration, print output | Icons for a product, if an icon library covers it. Figma vector tools handle simple custom icons. | Adobe official Illustrator tutorials, Nick Saporito free channel |
| **Adobe After Effects** | Paid | Marketing video, complex motion sequences, brand animation | In product motion. Figma smart animate plus Rive covers shipped UI motion. | School of Motion free lessons, Adobe official tutorials |
| **Rive** | Free tier | Shipped interactive animation with state machines, especially mobile | Static prototypes. | Rive documentation and community files, free |
| **Lottie via LottieFiles** | Free tier | Handing an animation to engineers as JSON | Anything a CSS transition can do. | LottieFiles docs |
| **Blender** | Free | 3D product renders, spatial UI, AR and VR concepts | Standard product design. | Blender Guru donut series, free, and Blender manual |
| **Spline** | Free tier | Interactive 3D on a web page, marketing sites | Product interfaces. | Spline docs and templates |
| **Framer** | Free tier | Shipping a marketing site or portfolio fast, high fidelity web prototypes | Product design files. | Framer Academy, free |
| **Webflow** | Free tier | Marketing site with a CMS, portfolio with real control | Product design. | Webflow University, free |
| **Penpot** | Free, open source | Team that cannot use Figma for licensing or self hosting reasons | Nothing else. Know it exists. | Penpot docs |
| **Sketch** | Paid | Joining a Mac only shop that never migrated | New work. | Sketch docs |
| **Adobe XD** | Discontinued | Nothing | Everything | Do not learn |
| **Canva** | Free tier | Internal decks quickly, social assets | Any product work. | Nothing needed |
| **Notion** | Free tier | Research repository, documentation, program admin | Design files. | Notion help docs |
| **Excalidraw** | Free | Fast throwaway diagrams in a call | Deliverables. | Nothing needed |
| **Miro** | Free tier | Workshops with clients who already use it | If FigJam is available. | Miro Academy |

**Verdict for this cohort.** Photoshop and Illustrator are marked optional and are assigned only if a sprint task genuinely needs raster retouching or custom vector icon work. Rive is assigned in Sprint 5 for one motion task because animation handoff is a real skill. After Effects, Blender, and Spline stay optional throughout.

## 11. AI tools

Allowed, disclosed, and never the decision maker.

| Tool | Cost | Useful for | Never for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini | Free tier | Divergent option generation, microcopy variants, reading unfamiliar code, summarising research transcripts you have already read | Deciding, or generating a research finding |
| Figma AI features | Included | Renaming layers, first pass content fill, search across files | Layout decisions |
| Cursor or VS Code with Copilot | Free tier | Reading a component, small token or Storybook edits | Shipping code you cannot explain |
| v0, Lovable, Uizard | Free tier | Throwaway concept scaffolds to react against | Deliverables |
| Whisper based transcription | Free | Interview transcripts | Synthesis, which is the designer's job |

**Rule.** Every artefact touched by AI carries a one line footer: what was used, and how the output was verified. An artefact that says "the model suggested X, I tested with three users and found Y" scores above one with no footer. Oral defense is the equaliser because the numbers defense cannot be outsourced.
