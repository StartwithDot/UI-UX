# Question Bank: Numbers Round

Draw six at random, in front of the designer. Rapid fire, 8 minutes, no notes, no file open.

These are answerable only by someone who built their own foundations and read the spec. That is the point. Nothing here can be reasoned out on the spot.

---

## Their own system

1. What is the contrast ratio of your body text against its background
2. What is your base type size and what ratio generated the scale
3. What is your smallest step in the spacing scale and what is it used for
4. How many steps are in your neutral ramp
5. Which token does your focus ring reference, primitive or semantic
6. What is the contrast ratio of your disabled text, and does it need to pass
7. What is your smallest interactive target in pixels
8. How many components are in the shared system right now
9. Which of your components has no disabled state and why
10. What is your line height for body text and why that number

## WCAG, criterion numbers required

11. What is the minimum contrast for body text at AA
12. What is the minimum contrast for large text, and what counts as large
13. What is the minimum contrast for a UI component boundary
14. What is the minimum target size at AA in WCAG 2.2
15. Which criterion covers focus appearance
16. Which criterion covers focus not being obscured by sticky elements
17. Which criterion covers colour not being the only means of conveying information
18. Which criterion requires that a label is visible, not only a placeholder
19. Which criterion covers redundant entry, asking for information twice
20. Which criterion covers accessible authentication and what does it prohibit
21. At what zoom level must content remain usable without horizontal scroll
22. What width must a page reflow to without two dimensional scrolling
23. How many times per second may content flash at most
24. What is the AA requirement for a time limit

## Metrics and the business

25. What is your primary metric and how is it calculated
26. What is your guardrail metric and what specifically does it protect against
27. If your primary metric went up 20 percent and your guardrail went up 5 percent, what would you recommend
28. What is the fee in this service and at which step is it collected
29. How long does the department's processing take, at the range stated in the brief
30. What is the cost to the citizen of one rejected submission, in money and in days

## The service

31. How many address fields does the live service ask for
32. What are the two update paths the live service offers
33. What is a URN and what is it needed for
34. Name the constraint that makes Lakshmi's case impossible to solve inside the interface
35. What is the document rule that Ramesh does not know
36. How many languages does your flow support and where does the control live

## Scoring

Right or wrong. No partial credit for "something like 4.5".

| Correct | Score | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 6 of 6 | 4 | Knows their system and the spec |
| 5 of 6 | 3 | Solid |
| 4 of 6 | 2 | Pass, with a named gap |
| 3 or fewer | 1 | Working inside a system they do not know. Say this plainly. |

## The pattern that matters

A designer who knows every WCAG number and none of their own token values has memorised a spec.

A designer who knows every token value and no WCAG numbers built by feel and checked nothing.

Both are 3 of 6 and they need opposite interventions. Record which half they missed, not only the count.

## Later sprint additions

Add these to the draw pool as the program progresses.

**Sprint 2:** sample size for the test you ran, what SUS measures and its range, task success rate you recorded, how many participants for a card sort to be meaningful.

**Sprint 3:** the four HEART dimensions, what an opportunity solution tree is for, your appetite in Shape Up terms, your funnel's biggest drop off percentage.

**Sprint 4:** how many tokens in each layer, which layer a screen may reference, what a component contribution requires before merge, the ARIA role of your most complex component.

**Sprint 5:** your model's stated latency, what confidence threshold you show a warning at, how many rows before your table needs virtualisation, target size in a dense table versus the AA minimum.

**Sprint 6:** your event count, your funnel conversion at each step, the minimum detectable effect of the test you designed, what your result would need to be to reverse your recommendation.
