# Sprint 4 Question Bank

## Numbers
1. How many tokens in each layer?
2. How many component tokens, and how is each justified?
3. What does a contribution need before merge?
4. How many forks did you find, and what is the cost?
5. What is your adoption metric, and its current value?
6. How many greys did the client have, and how many do you have?

## Reasoning
1. Where does the difference between the two brands live, and why?
2. What can your architecture not do?
3. Which component API property would you remove first, and what breaks?
4. How do you reject a contribution so the contributor accepts it?
5. What would you deprecate next?

## Adversarial
1. "You spent four weeks and shipped no features. Justify your existence."
2. "I need a component by Friday. Your process takes two weeks."
3. "Our team never chose this system. Why should we use it?"
4. "Your system makes my team slower. Prove otherwise."
