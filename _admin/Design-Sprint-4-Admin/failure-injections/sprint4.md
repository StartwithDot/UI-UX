# Sprint 4 Failure Injection

**Sealed until Week 15, Day 5, 10:00.**

Choose one scenario per designer, matched to the weakest point in their governance model (G1.1 to G1.6).

| # | Scenario | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bypass** | "The admin portal team shipped a custom date picker last sprint. It fails keyboard access. Your governance process did not stop it. What happened?" |
| 2 | **Blocked** | "The patient app team needs a component by Friday for a regulator demo. Your process takes two weeks. They want to know what the escape path is." |
| 3 | **Fork** | "The doctor console has quietly forked eleven of your components. Here is the diff. They say your system was too slow." |
| 4 | **Rejection backlash** | "A senior engineer says you rejected their contribution rudely, and told the Head of Engineering. They want to see your rejection criteria." |

## What a good response looks like

- Says which of their own rules caused it, by number
- Distinguishes a process failure from a communication failure
- Proposes a change to the governance model, with the cost
- Does not blame the team

## Weak

- Defends the process because it was followed
- Adds more process as the fix
- Writes a post mortem that blames people

Debrief Week 15, Day 6.
