# Program Flow

How the 24 weeks run and what the admin does when. Sprint-specific detail is in each `Design-Sprint-N-Admin/README.md`.

## Shape

6 sprints of 4 weeks. Two buffer weeks sit outside the 24: one after Sprint 3, one after Sprint 6.

| Sprint | Weeks | Project | Injection (Day 5 morning unless noted) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 to 4 | Aadhaar address update | Week 3: fee before upload, language on every screen |
| 2 | 5 to 8 | Skill scheme dropout | Week 7: two participants withdraw consent |
| 3 | 9 to 12 | Sutra, subscription flow | Week 11: a business demand against a stated principle |
| 4 | 13 to 16 | Three-product design system | Week 15: a governance failure |
| 5 | 17 to 20 | Capstone, four options | Week 19, Day 4: severe, individual |
| 6 | 21 to 24 | Portfolio, interview, market | Week 22: the external portfolio review |

## The six-day week

| Day | Block |
|---|---|
| 1 to 5 | LEARN (morning, 45 to 90 min), DO (afternoon, 1.5 to 4 h) |
| 4 | Critique, 90 minutes. Whiteboard why in week 3 of each sprint. |
| 5 | Defense in week 4 of each sprint |
| 6 | Cohort day: system, fundamentals, and the week's lab |

## Tracks running in parallel

- The **core** sprint tasks (C, R, P, S, F, X and the sprint-specific letters)
- The **labs**: `L-AI` (AI in design), `L-PR` (prototyping and responsive), `JR` (job readiness). Day 6.
- The **external bench**: critique, defense, portfolio or mock session each sprint (`external-bench.md`)

## Admin load

Core admin 6 to 8 hours a week plus about 3 hours of external coordination per sprint. Reviewer 3 to 4 hours. At five designers one person can hold both. Above eight, split them.

## Standing rules

1. Never design for them
2. Never approve a gate you have not checked
3. Never soften a defense score or a placement-readiness outcome
4. Deliver the injection on schedule
5. Read the reviews, not just the work
6. Review `09-Modern-Practice-Map.md` before every cohort
