# The External Bench

External people who do not know the cohort and owe it nothing. They are the source of the only unsoftened feedback in the program.

## Who

Keep a bench of at least five. Each must be a working product designer, design manager, or someone who hires designers. Recruit before Sprint 1 ends.

## What they do, per sprint (about 3 hours each)

| Sprint | Session |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sit in one critique and one defense |
| 2 | One critique, one defense, and the async JR2 review |
| 3 | One critique, one defense, JR3 live review and Mock interview 1 |
| 4 | One critique, one defense, Mock interview 2 (craft and critique) |
| 5 | The 20-minute presentation, one defense, Mock interview 3 |
| 6 | Portfolio review (week 22), mock loop (week 24), final defense, placement-readiness scoring |

Rotate: no designer should face the same external twice in a row.

## Briefing (send before every session)

> You will see one designer's work. You have not seen it before and you owe them nothing. Please ask what you would actually ask. Please score honestly using the sheet. Please do not be kind to be kind, and do not be cruel to be memorable. We will give you a short debrief afterwards.

## After

1. Collect the scorecards the same day.
2. Send a thank-you within 48 hours.
3. Ask: what did you see that we did not teach?

## Payment and thanks

Decide what you can offer (a fee, a donation, a reference, early access to graduates). Be honest about it up front. Do not make people work for free without saying so.
