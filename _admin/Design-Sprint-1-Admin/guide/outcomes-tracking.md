# Outcomes Tracking

The program does not promise jobs. It promises to track and publish what happens.

## Contact schedule

At 30, 90 and 180 days after week 24, ask each graduate (by message, five minutes):

1. How many applications have you sent since the program?
2. How many first interviews? How many second rounds?
3. How many offers, and at what kind of company and role? (Salary optional, never required.)
4. What is the single thing you would change about the program?
5. What did the program not prepare you for?

## Record

One row per graduate in a private spreadsheet. Never name graduates publicly without their consent. Publish aggregates (counts, ranges, time to first offer) to the next cohort's welcome pack, **whatever they are**.

| Code | Day 30 apps | Day 30 interviews | Day 90 apps | Day 90 interviews | Day 90 offers | Day 180 offers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Use

At each cohort's retro, read the answers to question 5 before changing any task. They are more reliable than your intuition about what to cut.
