# Review Quality

Rates a peer review in a pull request.

| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 4 | Names a specific thing that will break, who it breaks for, and a suggested direction. Cites a principle or measurement. |
| 3 | Names a specific issue and the user cost, no direction |
| 2 | A real observation, no cost, no principle |
| 1 | "Looks good", "nice", or taste ("I don't like the colour") |

A designer with three scores of 1 in a row is a rubber stamp and gets a conversation. A designer whose reviews are all 4 is probably the critique lead for the next round.
