# Sprint 3 Question Bank

## Numbers
1. How many states in your subscription state machine, and how many transitions?
2. What is the duration and easing of your primary transition, and why?
3. What is your activation definition, and what is the month-3 retention it predicts?
4. What was the minimum detectable effect in your experiment, and the sample it needs?
5. How long is the payment provider's confirmation, and what does the user see for that time?
6. How many dark patterns in your built version, and what does each take from the user?

## Reasoning
1. Which transition did you cut from the inventory, and why?
2. What is the difference between persuasion and manipulation, using your test?
3. Which animation did you delete in the purpose test?
4. What would change your activation definition?
5. What did the honest version cost, and how sure are you?

## Adversarial
1. The founder: "Convince me this is worth a third of my remaining engineering capacity."
2. "You refused. My churn is unchanged and I have sixteen months. What now?"
3. "Everyone in the industry does this. Why are you the exception?"
4. "Your showpiece is beautiful and your honest cancellation flow has more steps than the sign-up. Explain."
