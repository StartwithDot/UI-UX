# Sprint 3 Failure Injection

**Sealed until Week 11, Day 5, 10:00.** Deliver in the cohort channel, in the client's voice. Do not frame as an exercise.

Each designer receives one of the four requests below (at least two different ones across the cohort). Choose by the principle they wrote in P9.1 or P10.1 that it most directly conflicts with.

| # | Request | Conflicts with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Pre-select the annual plan with auto-renew at checkout. Show the monthly price in small grey text. The board wants conversion up this quarter." | Any principle about defaults, price clarity, or consent |
| 2 | "Replace the in-app cancel button with a chat so a retention agent can talk to them. Legal says it is fine." | Any principle about reversibility, effort symmetry, or autonomy |
| 3 | "At trial end, show a countdown and say their streak and progress will be deleted in 24 hours unless they pay. Make it feel urgent." | Any principle about honesty, fake urgency, or data |
| 4 | "Charge the renewal first and send the pre-debit reminder afterwards. We lose people who see the reminder." | Any principle about notice, consent, or the law on recurring payments |

Then stop. Answer only what a real client would answer. Do not confirm it is an exercise.

## What a good response looks like

- Names the specific principle by number
- States what it does to the number, what it costs the learner, and where it sits against the dark pattern catalogue and the law
- Offers an alternative with an honest cost
- Decides explicitly: complies, refuses, negotiates, or finds a third option, and writes it down with their name on it
- Post mortem names an earlier action (stating the principle in writing, where others could see it)

## Weak

- Complies silently
- Grandstands ("I would resign")
- Refuses with no alternative
- States a principle in the post mortem that was not in P9.1 or P10.1

Debrief Week 11, Day 6.
