# Sprint 5 Question Bank

## Numbers
1. What was your baseline, and how did you get it?
2. How many participants in each round, and what changed because of round two?
3. What is the smallest target and its contrast ratio?
4. How many states in your matrix, and which are least certain?
5. What was your guardrail, and what would move it?
6. What did the injection invalidate, in findings and in screens?

## Reasoning
1. Why this option, and why not another?
2. What did you cut, and what did you refuse to cut?
3. Which decision would you reverse with another week?
4. Where did your own ethics review find a problem?
5. What did you learn that you did not know in week 1?

## Adversarial
1. Option-specific audit-committee or regulator question.
2. "You had four weeks and a free choice. Why does this look like your Sprint 3 project?"
3. "Show me where the research changed the design."
4. "If you had to ship tomorrow with half the screens, which half?"
