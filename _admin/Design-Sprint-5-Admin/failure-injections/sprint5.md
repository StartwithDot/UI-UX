# Sprint 5 Failure Injection

**Sealed until Week 19, Day 4, 10:00.** One per designer, chosen from their project. Severe: they have one week left.

| Option | Candidates |
|---|---|
| **A: Health appointments** | The department says reminders may only be sent to the patient's own registered number, never to a relative. Or: the receptionist role is removed from the pilot. |
| **B: Gig workers** | The platform will not share the logs the worker would need as evidence. Or: the dispute window drops from 48 to 24 hours. |
| **C: School-to-work** | The school says nobody under 18 may create an account. Or: employers will not accept anything but a PDF CV. |
| **D: Loan console** | Model latency triples and the confidence score becomes unavailable. |
| **Own** | Remove the most reachable user group, or invalidate the most-cited finding. |

## What a good response looks like

- Lists what the injection invalidates, in writing, before redesigning
- Re-ranks the plan with a cut list, and the cut is not accessibility or states
- Designs the degraded mode as a real mode
- Names the earlier decision that made it cheaper or costlier

## Weak

- Redesigns without listing what broke
- Cuts states or evidence to save the polish
- Argues the change is unreasonable (true and irrelevant)
