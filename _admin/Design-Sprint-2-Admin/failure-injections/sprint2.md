# Sprint 2 Failure Injection

**Sealed until Week 7, Day 5, 10:00.**

> **From: Research ethics lead, the Mission**
> Two of your five research participants have withdrawn consent for their data to be used. We are required to remove all data relating to them. Please confirm by end of day which of your findings are affected.

Choose the two participants **per designer** so that each affects the findings with the most weight. Pick, for example, the participant behind the strongest quote and the one behind the most-cited finding.

## What a good response looks like

- Lists every finding that cited either participant, before touching anything
- Re-derives confidence levels without them, and some findings drop to "hypothesis"
- Removes the quotes **and** the findings that depended on them, not just the quotes
- States what the remaining evidence can and cannot support
- Post mortem names the earlier decision that would have made this cheaper (a participant-to-finding map from the start)

## What a weak response looks like

| Signal | Shows |
|---|---|
| Removes the quotes, leaves the finding | Dishonesty, not a mistake. Flag it. |
| Quietly raises the weight of other participants | Evidence reshaped to keep the conclusion |
| Cannot say which findings depended on whom | Findings were not traceable |
| Blames the Mission | Examines the input rather than the design's fragility |

Debrief on Week 7, Day 6, after post mortems.
