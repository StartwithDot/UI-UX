# Sprint 2 Question Bank

For the defense. Numbers, reasoning, adversarial.

## Numbers
1. How many participants did you interview, and how many tested?
2. What was your confidence level on your strongest finding, and what is it based on?
3. How many findings cite only one participant?
4. How many assumptions did you list, and how many did the research change?
5. What was the biggest interviewer talk-time ratio in your transcripts?
6. After the withdrawals, how many findings dropped a confidence level?

## Reasoning
1. Why did you choose this method for your riskiest assumption?
2. What would have proved your hypothesis wrong, and did you look for it?
3. Where did a participant contradict you, and what did you do?
4. Which finding do you trust least, and why is it in the report?
5. How did you decide severity ratings, and who disagreed?

## Adversarial
1. "Five people is not a sample. Why should I believe anything you found?"
2. "The Mission said it is a motivation problem. You have contradicted them with five interviews. Convince me."
3. "You removed two participants' data. How do I know the rest was not also shaped to fit?"
4. "Your design change cannot be traced to a finding. Why is it in the spec?"
