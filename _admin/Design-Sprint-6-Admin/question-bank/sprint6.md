# Sprint 6 Question Bank

Used by the panel. Questions are drawn from `Design-Sprint-6/07-interview-question-bank.md`; the admin supplies these additional probes.

## Numbers
1. How many applications sent, to whom, and how many responses?
2. How long does your portfolio take to load on a mid-range phone?
3. What is the accessibility score, and what did the tool miss?
4. How many words in each case study?

## Reasoning
1. Which project did you cut, and why?
2. What does your first screen claim, and what evidence is on the second?
3. Why this order of case studies?
4. What did the external reviewer say that hurt, and what did you change?

## Adversarial
1. "This looks like every portfolio I see. What is different?"
2. "How much of this did AI do?"
3. "I do not believe this result. Show me how you know."
4. "What are you still bad at, and what have you done about it this month?"
