# Placement Readiness

Scored by each external independently at the end of the week 24 mock loop and defense. Compare afterwards.

## Dimensions (1 to 4)

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **First screen** (90-second test) | Cannot say what the person does | Can say it after effort | Clear and specific | Clear, specific, and makes me want to read more |
| **Case study depth** | Process theatre | Decisions, no alternatives | Decisions, alternatives, evidence | Plus a real mistake and what it cost |
| **Craft, live** | Cannot critique or design without help | Gets there with prompting | Solid critique and a workable redesign | Sees the user cost first, specifies precisely |
| **Reasoning under pressure** | Bluffs or collapses | Holds on easy questions | Holds, concedes honestly | Changes mind for better evidence and says why |
| **Communication and honesty** | Overclaims, or cannot be understood | Uneven | Clear, owns attributions | Clear, owns attributions, volunteers limits |
| **Accessibility and systems fluency** | Vocabulary only | Can name criteria | Can state how they verified | Can fix a real example on the spot |

## Outcome

| Outcome | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Ready** | No dimension below 3, at least three at 4 from the majority of externals |
| **Ready, with a named gap** | At most one dimension below 3. Name it. |
| **Not yet** | Two or more dimensions below 3, or any 1 |

Externals can override with a written reason. The outcome is delivered in person with a written list of next steps (what to do, in what order, by when).

## Do not

- Soften the outcome in the room
- Promise that the outcome predicts offers. It does not.
- Show a designer another designer's scores.
