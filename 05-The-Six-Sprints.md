# 05 — The Six Sprints

The whole 24 weeks on one page. Read it once so you know where you are going, then forget it and work one week at a time.

---

## The map

| Sprint | Weeks | Project | Focus | What you leave with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 1–4 | Aadhaar address update, for a state government digital services unit | Visual craft, one complete flow | One rebuilt high-stakes flow, every state, accessibility passing |
| **2** | 5–8 | School scholarship application, for an education mission | Research and framing | 5 interviews, a usability study, findings with confidence levels |
| **3** | 9–12 | Fleet management platform for small transporters | Product thinking and metrics | A scoped feature with a defined activation metric and a tested prototype |
| **4** | 13–16 | Health tech company with three products and three systems | Design systems | A three-layer token set, two brands, documented components, governance |
| **5** | 17–20 | Loan underwriting console with a risk model | Complex and AI interfaces | A dense professional interface with every AI failure state designed |
| **6** | 21–24 | Your own project, chosen and agreed | Ship and measure | A shipped flow, a measured result, and a case study |

---

## Why this order

**Sprint 1 is craft, first, deliberately.** You cannot do research well if you cannot make a screen that works. Four weeks of type, colour, space, states, and accessibility comes before anything else because everything after it is built on top.

**Sprint 2 is research, second.** Now that you can build, you learn how to find out what to build. Sprint 1 gave you a flow you designed from a brief. Sprint 2 makes you go and check whether the brief was right.

**Sprint 3 is the business.** Craft plus research still does not make a product decision. This is where the number enters and where you first have to say what your design does to money.

**Sprint 4 is the invisible work.** Tokens, components, documentation, governance. Nobody compliments it. It is what holds products together, and it is a large share of the jobs available.

**Sprint 5 is the hard interface.** Density, expertise, keyboard-first, and a model whose output a human has to interpret without over-trusting. It uses everything from the first four sprints at once.

**Sprint 6 is proof.** One project, alone, from discovery to a measured result. The number might not be the one you hoped for, and reporting it honestly is the point.

## The four things that repeat every sprint

**1. Learn then do, daily.** Named source in the morning, task in the afternoon, summary committed.

**2. A failure gets injected mid-sprint.** Around week 3. You do not know what it is until it lands. A constraint changes, a participant withdraws, a metric moves, a technical requirement is rejected. You write a post mortem: what changed, what it broke, what it cost you, and what earlier decision would have made it cheaper.

**3. A gate blocks the finish.** States, accessibility, evidence. Checklists, not opinions.

**4. A defense ends it.** 55 minutes, six parts, in week 4. 90 minutes and seven parts in Sprint 6.

---

## The two things that carry across all 24 weeks

**The design system in `system/`.** Started in Sprint 1 week 1 with a type scale and a colour ramp. By Sprint 4 it is a real multi-brand token architecture with governance. By Sprint 6 it is handed to the next cohort. It is the same system the whole time, which means every shortcut you take in week 2 is still there in week 20. That is intentional.

**Your fundamentals folder.** 120 daily drill entries, 24 learning weeks of summaries, 24 teardowns, 6 explainers, and every why session you presented. At the end this is the only complete record of what you learned, and it is what the final reflection round is built from.

---

## What you have at the end

- 6 project case studies, one per sprint
- 1 shipped and measured project
- A design system you helped build and govern
- 24 teardowns of real products
- ~120 drill entries
- 6 passed defenses
- A written ledger of what you can demonstrably do

The portfolio is a by-product. The thing being built is the ability to defend a decision to someone who wants it to be wrong.
