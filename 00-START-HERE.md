# 00 — START HERE

You are in the DOTSET Product Design cohort. 24 weeks. 5 designers. 6 sprints of 4 weeks.

This file tells you what to read, in what order, and what to do in your first three hours. Read this file completely before opening anything else.

---

## 1. The one rule of this program

**You learn a thing, then you use it straight away, then you write down what you learned.**

Not: read for three weeks then design. Not: design for three weeks then read.

Every single day has two blocks:

| Block | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **LEARN** | 1 to 2 hours | A named chapter, a named video, a named page. Nothing vague. |
| **DO** | 1 to 2 hours | A task that uses exactly what you just learned. |

At the end of the LEARN block you write a short summary and commit it. At the end of the DO block you commit the task output. Both are checked.

If you skip the LEARN block, the DO task will be impossible and you will guess. Guessing is the thing this program exists to remove.

## 5. Where your work goes

Everything you produce is committed to git. Nothing lives only in Figma, and nothing lives only in your head.

| What you make | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Learning summary | `Design-Sprint-{s}/students/UX{n}/week{n}/session{n}-learning.md` |
| Task output | `Design-Sprint-{s}/students/UX{n}/week{n}/{TASK-ID}-{name}.md` |
| Figma screens | Figma is the canvas. Commit the link plus an exported PNG. |
| Anything shared by the cohort | the sprint's `system/` or `delivery/` |
| Weekly teardown and why session | the sprint's `fundamentals/` |
| Portfolio writing, reviews, interview scorecards | `students/UX{n}/portfolio/` |
| Your AI log | `students/UX{n}/ai-log.md` |

**One task, one file, one commit.** The commit message starts with the task ID, for example `C1.1 type scale with Devanagari test`.

## 6. How much time this takes

| Per week | Hours |
|---|---|
| Learning blocks, 5 days | 5 to 8 |
| Doing blocks, 5 days | 8 to 12 |
| Critique | 1.5 |
| Cohort day session | 1.5 |
| **Normal week** | **18 to 22** |
| Weeks with a lab (14 of the 24) | add 1.5 to 3 |

That is roughly three full days a week for 24 weeks, about 480 hours in total. It is not compatible with a full-time job. If you cannot give it, tell the admin before week 1 rather than at week 6.

If you are consistently over 24 hours, tell the admin. Something in the week is mis-sized and it gets fixed for everyone, not just for you.

---

## 7. Three things that will make you fail

**Skipping the learning block.** The task will take you four hours instead of one, and the result will be a guess. Every task in this program is written assuming you did the reading in that day's LEARN block.

**Not committing the learning summary.** It is 10 minutes of writing. It is also the only proof that you understood something, and it is what you will read back before the defense at the end of the sprint.

**Saving everything for one big push.** Work is committed as you finish each task. A single end of week dump cannot be reviewed, cannot be critiqued, and teaches you nothing about working in the open.
