# Sprint 6 — Read First

**Weeks 21 to 24 · Portfolio, interview, and getting hired**

---

## What changes

You stop designing products and start designing the evidence that you can design products.

This is the sprint most programs skip or do badly, and it is the one that decides whether the previous twenty weeks convert into a job. You have five projects, thousands of words of documented reasoning, real research with real participants, and five defended sprints. Almost nobody applying for a junior role has that. Right now it is all in a git repository that no hiring manager will ever open.

---

## The honest framing

A portfolio is not a gallery. It is an argument that you can be trusted with a problem, made to a stranger who will spend ninety seconds on it before deciding.

Everything in Sprint 6 follows from that sentence. Ninety seconds means the first screen has to carry the argument. A stranger means no shared context and no benefit of the doubt. An argument means the reasoning is the content, and the screenshots are the evidence.

## What is different

**The audience is external.** Everything this sprint is judged by whether a stranger with hiring authority would respond to it. Your peers' opinions matter less than they did.

**Week 24 involves real applications.** Not practice ones. You will send at least ten real applications to real openings, and you will track the responses. The program does not end with a hypothetical.

**The final defense is an interview.** Ninety minutes, run as a real design interview loop by someone who has not been your reviewer. Portfolio walk, whiteboard, craft, critique, and behavioural.

---

## The uncomfortable part

**You will cut two of your five projects.** Three case studies is the right number — enough to show range, few enough that each gets read. Choosing which two to leave out means admitting which of your work is weakest, and that is the exercise.

**You will be told your portfolio is not good enough**, by someone external, in week 22. That is the point of getting external review before you send applications rather than after.
