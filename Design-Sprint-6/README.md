# Design Sprint 6: Capstone

**Weeks 21 to 24. UX1 to UX5. The last four weeks.**

One problem each, taken from discovery to a measured result, alone. The cohort reviews, it does not collaborate.

## Read first

1. `docs/project-brief.md`
2. `docs/task-list.md`
3. `docs/capstone-requirements.md`
4. Your own week 1 work, all of it, before you choose a problem

## What ships by week 24

- A problem chosen and agreed, with a baseline number that existed before you started
- 5 research sessions, your own participants, findings with confidence levels
- A full flow, all states, both widths, gate passed, screen reader passed
- A handoff spec reviewed by another designer playing the engineer
- Instrumentation defined, and measured if it shipped
- **The result, reported honestly, including if it did not work**
- A portfolio case study that contains what you got wrong
- A 90 minute final defense, seven rounds, with an external panel

## The one thing that makes this sprint different

Every other sprint ended with work. This one ends with a number, and the number might not be the one you wanted.

**A negative result honestly reported scores higher than a positive result with no measurement.** Everyone claims their design worked. Almost nobody can show it. That gap is what these four weeks close.

## Route A or Route B

Route A is a real problem for a real organisation with real users and something that can ship. Route B is assigned by the admin for anyone who cannot secure a real one.

Route A is better and it has one trap: choosing something too big to ship. A shipped single flow with a measured result beats a beautiful unshipped project every time, at the defense and afterwards.
