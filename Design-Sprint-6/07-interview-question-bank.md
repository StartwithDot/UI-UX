# Interview Question Bank

108 questions, grouped the way a real loop is grouped, each with what the interviewer is actually testing. The surface question is almost never the real one.

Read the whole file in week 23. You will write answers to 40 of them (task B3.1). Choose the 40 so that every section is covered, and always include the three you are worst at.

**How to use the "testing" column.** Answer the testing column, not the question. "Walk me through your process" is testing whether you can adapt a process to a problem; a recited double diamond fails it.

## The three hardest, for most people

1. **"Why did you leave your last field?"** The answer is not an apology. It is a short, true account of what pulled you towards design, with evidence that you chose it.
2. **"What is your biggest weakness?"** Name a real fault, give an instance with a cost, describe the correction, and admit the correction takes effort.
3. **"What would you do if the business overruled you?"** Use your five-part ethics position, and say what you would write down.

## Questions you should ask them

An interview is two-way. Ask at least three of these, and write down the answers.

- What does a good first 90 days look like for this role?
- How do designers and engineers work together here? Can I see a recent handoff?
- How does design get measured, and who owns the number?
- What was the last decision design lost, and what happened?
- How do you give feedback on early-career designers' work?
- What would make you regret this hire in six months?
