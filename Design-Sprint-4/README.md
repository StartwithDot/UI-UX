# Design Sprint 4: Design Systems at Scale

**Weeks 13 to 16. UX1 to UX5.**

The project: **three products, one company, three design systems that do not agree**. A health tech company with a patient app, a doctor console, and an admin portal. Each was built by a different team. Nothing is shared, everything looks nearly the same, and nothing behaves the same.

This sprint is about the work nobody sees, which is why most designers cannot do it.

## What ships by week 16

- An audit of all three products with the real cost of the divergence, in engineering hours and in user harm
- A three layer token architecture: primitive, semantic, component
- Multi brand and multi theme from one token set, proven with two brands and light plus dark
- A component library with documented API, states, variants, and accessibility annotations
- A governance model: how something enters the system, who decides, how it is versioned, how it is deprecated
- A migration plan that a real team could execute without stopping feature work
- A contribution accepted from another designer, and one of yours accepted by them

## Why this sprint is where the cohort splits

The work is unglamorous and mostly writing. Designers who like it tend to become the people who hold products together. Designers who find it tedious usually discover in week 15 that their Sprint 1 to 3 work was held up by decisions someone else made.

## The system in this sprint is real

You are not designing a hypothetical system. You are extending and restructuring the one you have all been using since Sprint 1, which by now has three sprints of accumulated inconsistency in it. That inconsistency is your audit subject and you made it yourselves.
