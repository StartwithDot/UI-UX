# Three questions based on project brief

## Question 1
How can we help candidates quickly tell the difference between urgent exam dates, medical forms and general notices when they are all mixed together on the main home page?

**Who can answer:** Kerala PSC Administrator

**Time estimate:** 4 days

**Impact:** To ensure candidates never miss an important exam date when everything is piled together, the design must feature clear category tabs or filters at the top of the page so that the candidate can be able to navigate to their task


## Question 2
Where on the screen is the warning message that tells users they cannot change their application after submitting and is it big enough to stop people from making mistakes?

**Who can answer:** Kerala PSC Workflow Designer

**Time estimate:** 1 day

**Impact:** If the warning is easy to miss, the application may be rejected after the reviewing process and the candidate may loss the chance to appear the exam

## Question 3
Why do certain menu items like "Exam Marks" send users away to a completely different website and how do we stop them from getting lost or confused?

**Who can answer:** Kerala PSC Web Developer

**Time estimate:** 2 day

**Impact:** If users think the website is broken when they get redirected, the design must add clear labels or warnings so they don't get confused
