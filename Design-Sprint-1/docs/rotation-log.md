# Rotation Log, Sprint 1

Two positions rotate weekly. Every designer holds each position at least once per sprint. Filled in by the core admin at Monday kickoff, closed on Friday.

## Sprint 1 rotation

| Week | Critique lead | System owner | System focus that week | Builders |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | UX1 | UX2 | Token naming, foundations decision, Figma library setup | UX3, UX4, UX5 |
| 2 | UX2 | UX3 | Button, input, select, upload components | UX1, UX4, UX5 |
| 3 | UX3 | UX4 | Component fixes from usability findings, focus behaviour | UX1, UX2, UX5 |
| 4 | UX4 | UX5 | Documentation drift, changelog, dark mode tokens | UX1, UX2, UX3 |

UX5 holds critique lead in Sprint 2 week 1 so the cycle stays even across sprints. The doubling designer rotates each sprint. Sprint 2 opens with the order shifted by one.
